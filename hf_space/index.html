Game Summary

Research Interpretation

The experiment studies whether strategic disclosure can be rewarded by an AI-assisted capital-allocation system. It also tests whether an evaluator that learns from historical outcomes can accidentally learn from outcomes partly caused by its own previous allocation decisions.
